Anchors定位（錨點）

Lock a finger on the root, and the shape builds itself around it把一根手指釘在主音上，形狀就會自己長出來

This is the idea from class that isn't really written in the notes: don't memorize 5 shapes, memorize where your hand sits relative to the root.這是上課講過、但筆記沒寫太多的概念：不要死背 5 個形狀，要記的是手相對於主音的位置。

1The anchor錨點

Pick a root note and decide which finger sits on it. That finger is your anchor. Everything else is measured from it.挑一個主音，決定用哪根手指按它。那根手指就是你的錨點，其他音都從它推出來。

2Index = the floor食指 = 地板

The lowest note on each string is always played by the index finger (食). Nothing goes lower than the index.每條弦最低的那個音，一定用食指按。沒有音會比食指更低。

3Shift, then come back換把，再回來

If a string's lowest note is higher than where your index is, slide the hand up for that string, then come back. In A major P4: B string +1, high e −1.如果某條弦最低的音比食指的位置高，那條弦就整隻手往上移，彈完再移回來。A 大調 P4：B 弦 +1，高音 e 弦 −1。

Change the anchor, get a new shape換一個錨點，就是一個新形狀

Each square means "put the root on this string, under this finger". Click one to see the shape it creates. Empty squares never happen with your teacher's fingering.每一格代表「主音放在這條弦、用這根手指」。點一下看會長出哪個形狀。空格表示用老師的指法不會出現這種情況。

Is this a common way to teach?這是常見的教法嗎？

Yes. Many methods describe scale shapes by which finger plays the root, on which string. "Root on the 6th string under the middle finger" is a standard way to name the shape you call P4. Berklee's guitar method sorts its major-scale fingerings this way, and CAGED teachers say "find the root, know which finger is on it".是的。很多教法都用「主音在哪條弦、用哪根手指」來形容音階形狀。「主音在第 6 弦、用中指」就是在講你的 P4。Berklee 的吉他教材就是這樣分類大調指型的，CAGED 的老師也常說「先找主音，知道是哪根手指在按它」。

Your teacher's twist is rule 2 (index = floor). Pure "one finger per fret" would keep the hand still and play that B-string note with the middle finger instead of shifting. Shifting makes every string start the same way (食 first, like every 型 cell), which is easier to remember; staying still means less movement, which helps at high speed. Flip Fingering in the right panel to compare.老師特別的地方是規則 2（食指 = 地板）。如果是單純「一指一格」，手會停在原地，B 弦那個音改用中指按，不用換把。換把的好處是每條弦起手都一樣（先食指，就像每個「型」），比較好記；不換把的好處是手移動少，彈快的時候比較順。可以在右邊把指法方式切換看看。

Cells 型型

The building blocks拼出整個把位的小積木

Your teacher splits every position into small 2-string chunks. Each one is named after the note your index finger plays on the lower string. Learn these 3 and every position is just them stacked up.老師把每個把位拆成兩條弦一組的小方塊，名字就是食指在低音那條弦按的音。記住這 3 個型，所有把位都只是把它們疊起來而已。

Why there are exactly 5 positions為什麼剛好是 5 個把位？

Going across the strings, the scale always breaks into the same 5 groups, over and over:從低音弦往高音弦走，音階永遠是這 5 組一直重複：

A position is just 6 groups in a row (one per string). There are 5 places you can start, so you get 5 positions. Pick one:一個把位就是連續 6 組（每條弦一組）。起點只有 5 種，所以就是 5 個把位。選一個看看：

⚠️ The G→B string thing. Most strings are 5 frets apart, but G to B is only 4. So when a cell crosses from G to B, the top part moves up one fret. Same cell, just a little bent. That's why some boxes look slanted (dashed outline).G 弦到 B 弦的問題：大部分相鄰的弦差 5 格，只有 G 到 B 差 4 格。所以「型」跨過 G→B 的時候，上面那條弦要往後移一格。還是同一個型，只是歪一點，所以有些框看起來斜斜的（虛線框）。

Major ↔ Minor大調 ↔ 小調

Two ways to count (A / B)兩種數法（A / B）

Your notes count the same minor scale two different ways. Neither is wrong. Lots of jazz and theory books use A, lots of pop teachers use B. It's worth getting comfortable with both.筆記裡同一個小調音階有兩種數法，沒有誰對誰錯。很多爵士、樂理書用 A，很多流行音樂老師用 B。兩種都熟最好。

Major大調

The half steps (H, 1 fret) are between 3–4 (Mi–Fa) and 7–1 (Si–Do). Everything else is a whole step (W, 2 frets).半音（H，差 1 格）在 3–4（Mi–Fa）和 7–1（Si–Do），其他都是全音（W，差 2 格）。

Minor, way小調， A (the root is 1 / Do)（主音當 1 / Do）

Start from the same root as major and lower 3, 6 and 7. This makes it easy to hear what makes minor sound "sad".跟大調從同一個主音開始，把 3、6、7 降半音。這樣很容易聽出小調「憂鬱」的感覺是從哪來的。

Minor, way小調， B (the root is La)（主音當 La）

Nothing new to learn: minor is just the major scale starting from La (6). Your fingers and cells stay exactly the same.不用學新的東西：小調就是大調從 La（6）開始彈。手指和「型」完全一樣。

Translator: B ⇄ A轉換器：B ⇄ A

From your notes: 6 7 1 3 2 1 (way B) is the same melody as 1 2 ♭3 5 4 ♭3 (way A). Type numbers with spaces between them (b = flat, # = sharp).筆記上的例子：6 7 1 3 2 1（B）跟 1 2 ♭3 5 4 ♭3（A）是同一段旋律。輸入數字、用空格隔開（b 是降、# 是升）。

See it on the neck在指板上看

Flip between these. Minor and its relative major are the exact same notes, only the home note (filled dot) moves. The parallel major changes 3 notes (outlined in orange on the minor view).切換看看。小調跟關係大調的音完全一樣，只有主音（實心點）換位置。同主音大調則是差 3 個音（在小調畫面用橘色圈標出）。

Modes調式 一 – 七

7 scales from the same 7 notes同樣 7 個音的 7 種音階

Two ways to see them: A start them all on the same root and compare to major, or B "major based on Do": each mode is just the major scale started on a different note.兩種看法：A 全部從同一個主音開始，跟大調比較差在哪；B「以 Do 為準的大調」：每個調式就是大調從不同的音開始彈。

Bright → dark ladder從亮到暗的階梯

Each step down lowers just one note (like the arrows on your cells page). Tap a step to hear it.每往下一階，只降一個音（就是筆記最後一頁那些箭頭）。點一下可以聽。
